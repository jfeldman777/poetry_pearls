A CLEAR SHELL

Оригинал

Then fire burned my body to a clear shell. Thought whether the fanning tempest blew from hell Or heaven I could not, cannnot, tell - Who have no sense Left for so nice a difference.

But I learned the essential function of extreme pain - Of liquid fire pouring again and again and again And again through the horrified body: such pain Makes wholly innocent. Therefore am I impenitet

Today. Today ask no forgiveness, Having nothing to be forgiven. And my soul, no less House-proud than at the beginning, shows Death Smilingly over the place, Trusting this new face.
